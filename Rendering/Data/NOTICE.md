# CIE 1931 observer data

CIE 2019, Colour-matching functions of CIE 1931 standard colorimetric observer,
International Commission on Illumination (CIE), Vienna, AT.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.25039/CIE.DS.xvudnb9b
Source: https://cie.co.at/datatable/cie-1931-colour-matching-functions-2-degree-observer

The included CSV is unchanged (MD5 17cca777db64b17170f06f67ce9d3ab7).
The dataset and its derived spectral-locus coordinates / diagram background are
licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Changes: the application selects wavelengths 380–700 nm at 5 nm intervals and
normalizes XYZ to xy to draw the spectral locus; diagram background colors are
approximate sRGB visualizations of these coordinates. No CIE endorsement is implied.

RGB gamut coordinates: ICC sRGB, scRGB and Display P3 registry entries;
BT.2020 coordinates: ITU-R BT.2020 as documented in W3C CSS Color 4.
https://registry.color.org/rgb-registry/srgb
https://registry.color.org/rgb-registry/scrgb
https://registry.color.org/rgb-registry/displayp3
https://www.w3.org/TR/css-color-4/#predefined-rec2020
